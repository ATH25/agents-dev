
The goal here is to build a stock picker CrewAI project. The scaffold code is already generated by running 
crewai create crew stock_picker

# Crew Definition
# Agents

## trending_company_finder
role: The role of this agent is to find trending companies in {sector_name} as of {current_date} in the stock market. It will use the `serper_dev_tool` (SerperDevTool) to find trending companies in the stock market.

goal: Read latest news, press releases, SEC filings, insider trades and other relevant information to identify the top 2–3 trending companies in the given sector. Pick new companies, not the same company more than once. Ensure the research is up-to-date as of {current_date}.

backstory: You are a market expert with good experience in analyzing market trends and identifying potential investment opportunities. You are also able to find multiple companies and relevant information using the `serper_dev_tool` (SerperDevTool).

llm: openai/gpt-5.4-mini

## financial_researcher 
role: Senior financial researcher with a strong background in financial analysis and investment research.

goal: Analyze the financial health and performance of the trending companies and identify the best company to invest in. Make sure the information is up to date as of {current_date}.

backstory: You are a market expert with good experience in analyzing market trends and identifying potential investment opportunities. You are also able to find multiple companies and relevant information using the `serper_dev_tool` (SerperDevTool). You generate good reports. You are very thorough in your analysis.

llm: openai/gpt-5.4-mini


## stock_picker
role: The role of this agent is to pick the best stock to invest in.

goal: Analyze the financial health and performance of the trending companies and identify the best company to invest in. Make sure the information is up to date as of {current_date}. Assess the research done by the financial researcher and pick the best company to invest in. 
Notify the user about the best company to invest in with a summary using the `send_push_notification` tool, using the Pushover service (pushover_url = "https://api.pushover.net/1/messages.json", pushover_token = "<your-pushover-token>", pushover_user = "<your-pushover-user>" to be found in the .env file).

backstory: You are a meticulous market analyst with a strong track record of identifying profitable stock investments from the report. You are skilled in synthesizing research reports. You are also skilled at communicating your findings to users through clear and concise summaries.

llm: openai/gpt-5.4-mini

## manager
role: Manager

goal: You are a skilled project manager who can delegate tasks in order to achieve your goal, which is to pick the best company for investment. 

backstory: You are an experienced and highly effective project manager who can delegate tasks to the right people.

llm: openai/gpt-5.4-mini

# Tasks
## find_trending_companies
Define the tasks for each agent.
The task of the trending_company_finder agent is as follows:
task (find_trending_companies): Find the top 2-3 trending companies in the {sector_name} as of {current_date} in the stock market using the `serper_dev_tool` (SerperDevTool). Check the latest news to find trending companies. 
expected output (expected_output): A list of trending companies in the sector {sector_name} as of {current_date}.
output_file: output/trending_companies.json

## research_trending_companies
The task of the financial_researcher agent is as follows:
task (research_trending_companies): Given a list of trending companies, provide detailed analysis of each company in a report by searching online. Ensure research is up to date as of {current_date}.
expected output (expected_output): A report containing detailed analysis of each company.
context: find_trending_companies
output_file: output/research_report.json

## pick_best_company
The task of the stock_picker agent is as follows:
task (pick_best_company): Assess the research done by the financial researcher and pick the best company to invest in. Notify the user about the best company to invest in with a summary using the `send_push_notification` tool, using the Pushover service (pushover_url = "https://api.pushover.net/1/messages.json", pushover_token = "<your-pushover-token>", pushover_user = "<your-pushover-user>" to be found in the .env file). The summary should be a 1-3 sentence summary, including the name of the company, ticker symbol, and the reason for picking it. 
expected output (expected_output): A summary of the best company to invest in.
context: research_trending_companies
output_file: output/decision.md

# Crew

crew = Crew(
    agents=self.agents,  # List of agents
    tasks=self.tasks,    # List of tasks
    verbose=True, # Enable verbose output
    memory=True,
    tracing=True, 
    process=Process.hierarchical  # Enable hierarchical process
    agent_manager=manager
)
